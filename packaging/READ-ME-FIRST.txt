MEDIA DOWNLOADER - PLEASE READ BEFORE OPENING

1. Drag Media Downloader into the Applications folder shown here.

2. The first time you open it, macOS will block it. This is normal.
   The app is not signed with an Apple Developer certificate, which
   costs 99 dollars a year. It does not mean anything is wrong with it.

   On macOS 15 Sequoia or newer
     a. Double click the app. macOS says it cannot verify it. Click Done.
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy and Security.
     c. Scroll down to Security. Next to the message about Media
        Downloader being blocked, click Open Anyway.
     d. Confirm with Touch ID or your password, then click Open Anyway.

   On macOS 14 Sonoma or older
     a. Right click the app and choose Open.
     b. Click Open in the dialog.

3. After that first time, it opens normally by double clicking.

Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
